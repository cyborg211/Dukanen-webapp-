# DUKANEN (دكانين)

Dukanen is a mobile-first, multi-vendor African marketplace being built initially for South Sudan, with architecture intended to expand across East Africa.

## Product vision

**Buy. Sell. Discover.**

Dukanen connects buyers, individual sellers, small businesses, shops, service providers, and marketplace administrators in one trusted marketplace experience.

## MVP priorities

1. Homepage
2. Marketplace browsing
3. Product listings and product details
4. Search, categories, filters and location discovery
5. Authentication and role-based access
6. Seller onboarding and storefronts
7. Seller dashboard and product management
8. Favorites and reviews
9. Admin dashboard and moderation
10. Orders and notifications
11. Payment-ready architecture

## Design direction

- Primary green: `#0B6B3A`
- Secondary green: `#168A4A`
- Accent gold: `#F4C542`
- Background: `#F8FAF8`
- Dark: `#17201A`
- Muted: `#66736B`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

The product should be fast, accessible, low-bandwidth friendly, mobile-first, locally relevant, and portable between hosting/development environments.
